How it works

  1. 1 Open Blockbench, glTF/GLB or OBJ.
  2. 2 Pose Move bones or play an animation.
  3. 3 Pick a look Backdrop, light and grading in one go.
  4. 4 Render Export the shot and show it off.

Questions

Does my model get uploaded?

No. ModelShot opens and renders everything inside your browser. A file only leaves your device if you choose to share it.

Which files can I open?

Blockbench models, glTF and GLB, OBJ, and player skins. Cubes, meshes and partly transparent planes all work.

What do I need to run it?

A current desktop browser with WebGL2. No install, no account.

Why does Render take longer than the preview?

The preview is realtime. Render path-traces real bounce light and soft shadows, then hands you the finished image.
